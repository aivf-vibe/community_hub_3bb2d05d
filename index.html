Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Executive Circle

Who is eligible to join Executive Circle?

Membership is exclusively for C-suite executives (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CMO, etc.) of Fortune 500 companies. Each application undergoes rigorous verification including company verification, role confirmation, and background checks.

How is privacy maintained?

We employ zero-knowledge architecture, end-to-end encryption, and multi-factor authentication. All communications are encrypted, and member data is never shared with third parties. Our platform is SOC 2 Type II certified.

What makes this different from LinkedIn?

Unlike public platforms, Executive Circle is invitation-only with verified Fortune 500 C-suite members. We provide exclusive insights, private events, and strategic partnership opportunities unavailable on public networks.

Are there membership fees?

Yes, annual membership fees ensure exclusivity and fund premium features. Fees vary by company size and member level. Contact our membership team for detailed pricing.

Apply for exclusive access to the world's most powerful executive network. Membership is by invitation only after rigorous verification.
